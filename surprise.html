My Dearest Agness,

I don't know if words will ever be enough to explain what you mean to me, but I wanted to try.

There is something beautiful about having someone whose presence can make an ordinary day feel a little more meaningful. Somehow, you have become that person to me.

I may not always have the perfect words, and I may not always know how to express everything I feel, but I hope you never doubt that you hold a special place in my heart.

If life were a book, I would not ask for every chapter to be perfect. I would simply be grateful if some of its most beautiful pages had you in them.

I created this little world not because a website could ever capture how special you are, but because I wanted to give you something that carries a little piece of my heart.

So whenever you visit this page, remember that somewhere, someone is smiling simply because you exist.

Thank you for being you, Agness. You are a beautiful part of my story.

♥ ✦ ♥
With affection, always.
Someone who thinks the world of you. ❤️
